# Financial Performance Dashboard | Power BI

An interactive 3-page Power BI dashboard designed to analyse business sales, profitability, and monthly performance using the Financial Sample dataset (September 2013 – December 2014).

**Author:** Olowookere Rodiyah  
**Tools:** Power BI Desktop, DAX, Data Modelling

---

## Project Overview

This project focuses on transforming raw financial data into meaningful business insights through an interactive Power BI dashboard.

The dashboard provides an overview of business performance, helping stakeholders understand revenue generation, profitability, product performance, and sales trends across different countries and customer segments.

## Project Objectives

The primary objectives of this project are to:

- Evaluate overall sales and profitability.
- Identify the products, countries, and customer segments contributing most to business performance.
- Analyse monthly sales and profit trends.
- Examine the impact of discounts on business profitability.
- Identify areas of concern and recommend possible improvements.

## Dashboard Structure

The dashboard consists of three interactive pages:

### 1\. Executive Overview

Provides a snapshot of the overall business performance through key performance indicators, monthly sales trends, and sales distribution across countries and products.

**Key metrics:**

- Gross Sales
- Net Sales
- Cost of Goods Sold (COGS)
- Profit Margin
- Units Sold

### 2\. Sales Analysis

Explores sales performance across different products, countries, and customer segments. It also examines quarterly trends, month-over-month growth, and discount distribution.

### 3\. Profit Analysis

Focuses on profitability by examining profit contributions across countries, products, and customer segments, alongside monthly profit trends.

**Interactive Features:**

- Year slicer
- Country slicer
- Product slicer
- Segment slicer
- Page navigation buttons

## Key Business Insights

### 1\. Government Segment Drives Profitability

The Government segment contributes approximately $53M in sales and $11.4M in profit, accounting for about 67% of total business profit.

Small Business follows with $42M in sales and $4.1M in profit.

This indicates a significant dependence on the Government segment, creating a potential concentration risk.

### 2\. Enterprise Segment Records a Loss

Despite generating approximately $20M in sales, the Enterprise segment records a loss of $0.6M.

This suggests a need to investigate its pricing structure, discount practices, and operating costs.

### 3\. Smaller Segments Show Strong Profit Margins

Channel Partners and Midmarket generate relatively small sales volumes but demonstrate strong profitability.

Although their contribution is currently limited, these segments present potential opportunities for business expansion.

### 4\. Paseo Leads in Sales While Amarilla Has the Highest Profit Margin

Paseo is the leading product, generating approximately $33M in sales and $4.8M in profit.

However, Amarilla records the highest profit margin at approximately 15.6%, compared with Paseo's 14.5%.

This highlights the difference between generating high sales and converting sales into profit efficiently.

### 5\. Profit Contribution Is Relatively Balanced Across Countries

France leads with approximately $3.8M in profit, followed by Germany at $3.7M and Canada at $3.5M.

The relatively small difference between the highest and lowest-performing countries suggests that profitability is distributed fairly evenly across the markets.

### 6\. Monthly Performance Shows Significant Fluctuations

Sales performance varies considerably from month to month, with noticeable fluctuations in monthly growth.

October 2014 records a significant sales spike, followed by a decline in November and a recovery in December.

December also records the highest monthly profit at approximately $2M.

### 7\. Discounts Have a Significant Impact on Profitability

Total discounts amount to approximately $9M, representing a substantial portion of gross sales.

This highlights the importance of monitoring discount policies to ensure that sales growth does not come at the expense of profitability.

## Business Recommendations

Based on the findings, the following actions are recommended:

| Priority | Recommendation |
| --- | --- |
| High | Review Enterprise pricing, discounts, and cost structure to address its negative profitability. |
| High | Reduce dependence on the Government segment by developing opportunities in other customer segments. |
| Medium | Strengthen discount management to protect profit margins. |
| Medium | Explore opportunities to expand the Channel Partners and Midmarket segments. |
| Medium | Review the pricing and cost structures of Velo and Carretera to improve their profit margins. |
| Medium | Improve planning around monthly sales fluctuations, particularly during the fourth quarter. |
| Low | Investigate differences in profitability across countries to identify opportunities for improvement. |

## Data Notes and Limitations

- The dataset contains 700 transactions covering 16 months.
- The 2013 reporting period covers only September to December, making direct year-over-year comparisons less reliable.
- Some segment-level findings are based on relatively small sales volumes.
- Profit margins presented in the insights are approximate because they are calculated from rounded dashboard figures.
- Profit Margin is calculated as Total Profit divided by Net Sales.
